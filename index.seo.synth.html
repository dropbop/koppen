Köppen-Geiger Interactive Map

Explore global Köppen-Geiger climate zones across historical periods from 1901–2020 and CMIP6 projections through 2099, with scenario switching, basemap controls, and click-to-inspect location details.

About the data and map coverage

The map includes the 1901–1930, 1931–1960, 1961–1990, and 1991–2020 historical periods, plus projected 2041–2070 and 2071–2099 climates under SSP1-1.9, SSP1-2.6, SSP2-4.5, SSP3-7.0, SSP4-3.4, SSP4-6.0, and SSP5-8.5.

Climate data is based on Beck et al. (2023), High-resolution (1 km) Köppen-Geiger maps for 1901–2099 based on constrained CMIP6 projections . Approximate place names are provided via OpenStreetMap Nominatim reverse geocoding.
